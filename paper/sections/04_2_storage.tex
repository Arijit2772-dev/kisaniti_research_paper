\subsection{Storage Rule on Simulated Scenarios}
\label{sec:results-storage}

The node has not run in real grain, so the rule was tested on \kn{stor_scenarios} simulated scenarios built from the monthly climate
normals of the IMD station at Patiala~\cite{imd2022}, each with \kn{stor_seeds} noise seeds and sensor biases of $\pm2$\,\%RH. Without
bias it agreed with the intended category in \kn{stor_s3_demo_agree}--\kn{stor_s2_demo_agree}\,\% of known hours in the four in-grain
scenarios and changed category at most \kn{stor_demo_flips_max} times a week; the latest-reading rule changed
\kn{stor_instant_flips_min}--\kn{stor_instant_flips_max} times, and shed-air humidity was a poor stand-in for the grain. The costs are
delay (the 24-hour median reported HIGH about \kn{stor_s2_demo_highdelay}\,h late) and unknowns. A $\pm2$\,\%RH sensor error mattered as
much as the choice of rule (up to \kn{stor_demo_falsehigh_biasplus_max}\,\% false HIGH hours), so a salt calibration is required before
real readings are interpreted. This measures how far noise and shortcuts move a rule from its definition, not whether the
definition predicts spoilage.
