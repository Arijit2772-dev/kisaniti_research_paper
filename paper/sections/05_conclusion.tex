\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Selling decisions of an FPO depend on whether the crop's price is set by policy or by the market. For Paddy (Common) in Punjab it
is set by policy: the announced support price was the most accurate forecast, no learned model beat the last price, and the useful
work is to share mandi limits fairly. The exact allocation raised the smallest share a farmer receives from
\kn{allpol_p1_nearest_min_fulfilment_pct}\% to \kn{allpol_kisaniti_min_fulfilment_pct}\% in the synthetic experiment and recovered part
of what a greedy rule gives up. For basmati the forecast comparison was fixed in advance, and its result is negative: on the
held-out season no method of eight beat the naive value at 1, 7 or 14 days after correcting for multiple comparisons
(Section~\ref{sec:results-basmati}), so for the system the baseline is enough, although a short-horizon gain for 1121 cannot be excluded. The five stages from data to recorded sales were built as one system and tested end to end.

\paragraph{Scope.} The price index is a market summary rather than the price a farmer received, and it covers September to January over
five seasons, so only large forecast gains are detectable. The portal check covers \kn{hc_rows} stratified rows, and the portal has no
second source. The allocation experiment uses a synthetic FPO, so it shows properties of the method rather than an income gain, and the storage
rule was evaluated on simulated scenarios. Wheat held after procurement is outside the scope, because Punjab mandis report too few wheat prices
outside procurement.

\paragraph{Future work.} The next steps are a one-week sensor run in stored grain; hold-or-sell advice with forecast ranges; the allocation
comparison with basmati prices and measured transport costs; and a deployment with one FPO after ethics approval, including a comprehension
test of the messages with farmers, delivery tracking through a messaging-platform account, a review of the off-plan sale rule with FPO staff
and a legal review of privacy.
