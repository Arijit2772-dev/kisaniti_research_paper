\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Selling decisions of an FPO depend on whether the crop's price is set by policy or by the market. For Paddy (Common) in Punjab it
is set by policy: the announced support price was the most accurate forecast, no learned model beat the last price, and the useful
work is to share mandi limits fairly. The exact allocation raised the smallest share a farmer receives from
\kn{allpol_p1_nearest_min_fulfilment_pct}\% to \kn{allpol_kisaniti_min_fulfilment_pct}\% in the synthetic experiment and recovered part
of what a greedy rule gives up. For basmati the forecast comparison was fixed in advance, and its result is negative: on the
held-out season no method of eight beat the naive value at 1, 7 or 14 days after correcting for multiple comparisons
(Sect.~\ref{sec:results-basmati}), so for the product the baseline is enough. The five stages from data to recorded sales were
built as one system and tested end to end, including an approval that reserves stock exactly and a way for the manager to send each
farmer the message from his own WhatsApp.

\paragraph{Limits.} The index is a market summary, not the price a farmer received, and exists only from September to January over five
seasons, so only large improvements can be detected. The cleaned prices have not passed the hand check. The FPO in the allocation experiment
is synthetic, so no income gain is claimed. The storage sensor was tested on simulated scenarios only. Held wheat is not forecast because
Punjab mandis report too few wheat prices outside procurement. The WhatsApp hand-over cannot see delivery, the Punjabi and Hindi texts have
not been read by a native speaker, off-plan sales follow a provisional rule, and no user study has been done.

\paragraph{Future work.} A one-week sensor run in grain; a hold-or-sell rule with ranges evaluated per season; the allocation comparison with
basmati prices and real transport costs; a deployment with one FPO after ethics approval, native-speaker review of the messages and a legal
review of the privacy controls; and the official messaging channel, so that one action reaches every farmer.
