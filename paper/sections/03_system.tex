\subsection{Forecasting Method}
\label{sec:method-forecast}

Figure~\ref{fig:method} summarizes how we compare forecasters. Each test season is forecast by
models trained only on earlier seasons, and the season just before it is held out to tune
settings such as the number of neighbouring mandis~$k$ in the graph and the ridge penalty.
This gives three forward-chaining folds with test seasons \kn{test_seasons}. Errors are
scored only on prices that a mandi actually reported.
