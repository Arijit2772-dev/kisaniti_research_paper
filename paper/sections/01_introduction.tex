\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) is a registered body of farmers that can pool, market and sell its members' produce~\cite{sfac}.
Selling for the group means choosing a mandi, a day and a quantity for each member's lot, and these choices are linked. The
best-paying mandi may be the farthest one. Waiting for a better price keeps grain in storage longer. When many members bring grain in
the same week, a single mandi can receive more than it can handle in a day. Storage is also one of the causes of India's post-harvest
losses, which a 2024 estimate of the Indian Council for Research on International Economic Relations put at about 1.53 trillion Indian
Rupees (Rs) of food a year~\cite{icrier}.

Most of the price information an FPO needs is public. The Agricultural Marketing Information Network (AGMARKNET)~\cite{agmarknet}
publishes the daily arrivals and the minimum, maximum and modal price reported by each mandi. The government announces a minimum support
price (MSP) for paddy and wheat before each season~\cite{msp1}\cite{msp2}. How much prices move depends on the crop. In the
\kn{mandis} Punjab mandis studied here, at least \kn{msp_share_min}\% of the Paddy (Common) prices reported in every season from
\kn{season_first} to \kn{season_last} were exactly the Grade-A MSP. Basmati paddy has no MSP. It sells at market prices that differ by
variety, so for a basmati farmer the choice of mandi and day changes what the crop earns.

KisanNiti is a decision-support system for FPO staff around Patiala. It reads AGMARKNET prices~\cite{agmarknet}, the FPO's lot records
and a grain-storage sensor, proposes a selling plan, lets a manager review it, and tells each farmer in their own language. The paper
makes five contributions. A simple regime test, the share of reported prices equal to the MSP, separates price-bound crops from market
crops (Section~\ref{sec:results-msp}). For basmati, eight forecasting methods were compared under a protocol fixed in advance and
evaluated once on a held-out season (Section~\ref{sec:results-basmati}). An exact allocation model shares the daily mandi limits fairly
among members and is compared with simpler rules (Section~\ref{sec:results-alloc}). A storage-sensing rule is evaluated on simulated
scenarios (Section~\ref{sec:results-storage}). Finally, the five stages from data to recorded sales are built and tested as one system
(Section~\ref{sec:results-system}). Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes the paper and outlines future work.
