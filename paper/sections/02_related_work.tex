\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Price forecasting.} Most Indian studies take daily or monthly AGMARKNET prices~\cite{agmarknet} and compare one model family
with autoregressive integrated moving average (ARIMA) models. The largest comparison covers 23 commodities in 165 markets from 2010 to
2024. In it, long short-term memory (LSTM) and gated recurrent unit (GRU) networks had the lowest errors~\cite{manogna}. Adding rainfall
and temperature helped deep models forecast tomato, onion and potato prices~\cite{nayak}. Two studies are closer to the crops here. Bhoi
et al. fitted six time-series models to monthly basmati prices from 74 Punjab markets and reported the forecasts as probability
bands~\cite{bhoi2023}. Balantrapu et al. studied prices anchored to the MSP and found that for wheat a naive forecast was almost as good
as their best model~\cite{balantrapu2026}. Graph models forecast many related series at once~\cite{ozden}\cite{min}; the temporal graph
convolutional network (T-GCN) and its attention-based variant (A3T-GCN) were built this way for traffic~\cite{zhao}\cite{bai}.

\paragraph{Storage and sensing.} Measured farm storage losses in North India are small in the first months after harvest. They were
0.39 to 0.50\% for wheat in Madhya Pradesh and zero for paddy in Punjab, where farmers sell soon after harvest, and they are expected to
rise after about three months~\cite{gulati2024report}. Low-cost monitors log temperature and humidity and raise alarms~\cite{islam}.
None of these systems passes its readings on to a selling decision.

\paragraph{Allocation and fairness.} Mixed-integer models already plan agricultural logistics for small growers, cooperatives and
food-grain freight~\cite{urra}\cite{anoop}. They minimize cost or maximize profit and do not consider how the gains are shared.
Leximin (lexicographic max-min) methods address exactly that~\cite{ogryczak2014}\cite{chen2023fairness}, and they have been used to
allocate school classrooms with real data~\cite{kurokawa2018}. None of the agricultural allocation papers read for this study used a
fairness criterion.

\paragraph{Advice to farmers.} Evidence on phone-based advice is mixed. In 100 Maharashtra villages, Short Message Service (SMS) price
and weather messages produced no significant change in the prices farmers received~\cite{fafchamps2012}.

Table~\ref{tab:related} lists only what each paper reports. Across the 38 studies reviewed, price forecasting, storage monitoring and
allocation were studied separately. No study fed a forecast into a plan that shares mandi capacity among the members of an FPO, and none
combined such a plan with a fairness rule or with a manager's approval before farmers are told. KisanNiti targets this combination in
Punjab, where basmati is the main crop sold at market prices and nearly all reported prices of common paddy equal the MSP.
