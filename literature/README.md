# Literature review (phase R1, EXP-00)

**Goal:** verified related work and a peer-comparison table for the paper.

**Method:**
- databases: Springer Link, ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore, Google Scholar;
- exact search strings and dates recorded;
- inclusion and exclusion rules.

**Topics:**
- Indian mandi price forecasting (AGMARKNET);
- graph and foundation models for crop prices;
- MSP procurement and farmer selling in Punjab;
- hold-or-sell and storage timing;
- on-farm storage losses in North India;
- low-cost grain storage IoT;
- fair allocation (leximin);
- FPO and cooperative decision-support systems.

**Output files:**
- `LITERATURE_REVIEW.md`: a table of 20–40 papers, recording for each:
  - year, region and crops;
  - data;
  - methods;
  - optimisation and fairness;
  - IoT;
  - communication;
  - human approval;
  - evaluation;
  - open code.
- `references.bib`, where every entry has a DOI or URL that was opened and checked.
- `verification_log.md`: who checked what and when.

**Also find sourced values for:**
- storage loss per month;
- holding cost;
- the crop-loan interest rate.

The v2 paper's 25 references must be re-verified the same way.

**Paper use:**
- Section 2 Related Work, in the chronological style of the reference paper but with complete Springer-format references;
- the feature comparison matrix;
- the novelty statement, taken from the gaps found, never assumed.
