## Layer 3: optimiser

- Method: staged exact lexicographic MILP: (1) maximise allocated quantity, (2) leximin on farmer fulfilment ratios, by the cumulative formulation of Ogryczak (maximise the sum of the k smallest ratios for k = 1..n, fixing each value before the next stage), (3) storage priority (HIGH then MEDIUM lots earliest), (4) maximise net return, (5) deterministic tie-break. Quantities in grams, money in exact fractions; fairness values are recomputed exactly from the integer plan after each stage, never taken from the solver's float.
- Brute-force oracle vs solver (3 back ends, 2 methods): 1,728 of 1,728 agree (rerun today, identical apart from timings); **360 further solver runs on 120 never-used instances (seeds 90000+, `optimizer_fresh_seeds.json`): 360 of 360 agree.**
- Independent validator vs a second rule checker: 4,431 of 4,431 random edits agree.
- Policy comparison (60 plans, 240 exact solves, all proven optimal), sensitivity grid (9 settings), scaling grid (10 to 1,000 farmers): re-checked against the result files by `verify_claims.py`.
- Found and fixed today: the paper did not say that distances are straight-line times a road factor of 1.3 (now stated).
